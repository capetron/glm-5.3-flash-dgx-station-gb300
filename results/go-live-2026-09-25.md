# Go-live measurements, 2026-09-25

Hardware: one NVIDIA DGX Station GB300. Service: exactly `systemd/glm53-flash.service` in this repo.
Harness: burst per concurrency level, 256-token answers, 3 repeats after a warm-up, reasoning-aware time to first
token. Prompts were short (median 39 to 40 prompt tokens), so these are decode numbers, not long-context numbers.

| Users at once | Aggregate tok/s | Per user, median tok/s | Time to first token, p50 | Failures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 140.9 | 145.9 | 0.061 s | 0 |
| 32 | 1,462.1 | 55.6 | 0.985 s | 0 |

Values shown are the first repeat; the service runbook records 145.9 tok/s for one user and 1,464 tok/s aggregate
at 32 users across the repeats.

Quality gate before go-live: tool-call battery 38 of 38, agent battery 15 of 15 (vendor sampling settings).
Smoke test: a `get_weather` request came back as a structured `tool_calls` entry.

Longer-context and per-configuration results (prefill by prompt length, needle tests, other quantizations) are on
the [GLM-5.3-Flash benchmark page](https://petronellatech.com/ai/llm-benchmarks/models/glm-5-3-flash/).
